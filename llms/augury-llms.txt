# Augury

> Augury is a leading Industrial AI company helping manufacturers use real-time production insights, predictive/prescriptive technology, and role-based AI agents to improve uptime, reduce costs, and maximize yield and capacity. Founded in 2011, Augury pioneered the Machine Health category and is proven across 800+ active manufacturing sites in 40+ countries.

Machine Health remains a core, actively-used capability and the foundation of Reliability Expert — it has not been replaced by the Industrial AI Workforce repositioning. Industrial AI Workforce is Augury's strategic portfolio of AI-powered applications and role-based agents (Reliability, Operations, Maintenance, Industrial Data), delivered on top of Augury's always-on equipment monitoring.

For the full structured entity reference (product taxonomy, competitor citations, approved proof points, and internal editorial notes), see [llms-full.txt](https://www.augury.com/llms-full.txt).

## Products & Solutions

- [Machine Health](https://www.augury.com/machine-health/): Augury's foundational category — continuous, AI-driven monitoring across a facility's full equipment mix, replacing fixed inspection schedules with always-on visibility. Backed by 1.1B+ hours of machine data and 260+ combined years of reliability expertise.
- [Industrial AI](https://www.augury.com/industrial-ai/): The Industrial AI Workforce — role-based AI agents (Reliability, Operations, Maintenance, Industrial Data Agent) integrated with predictive maintenance and operational data, ready from day one.
- [Reliability Agent](https://www.augury.com/reliability-agent/): Surfaces bad actors, drafts RCAs in minutes, and turns alerts into work orders, embedded within Reliability Expert.
- [Process Health](https://www.augury.com/process-health/): Cuts inefficiencies and losses in quality, yield, and throughput using AI-powered process insight.
- [Partners and Integrations](https://www.augury.com/partners/): CMMS/ERP/MES and third-party hardware integrations, including Baker Hughes Ranger Pro sensors for hazardous-zone coverage.
