Key highlights: What are AI agents in manufacturing? AI agents in manufacturing are autonomous systems that integrate with your existing tools, synthesize operational data, and act on behalf of a specific persona, such as a maintenance manager or a supply planner. Think about how agentic technology impacts manufacturing operations.
