Key highlights: What is predictive maintenance analytics? Predictive maintenance analytics combine machine data, maintenance history, and operating context with artificial intelligence (AI) to assess asset risk before failure. You get a clear picture of how your equipment is behaving, what past repairs suggest, and which new issues are forming.
