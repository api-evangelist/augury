What is condition-based monitoring? Condition-based monitoring is a manufacturing maintenance strategy that utilizes continuous sensor data (such as vibration, temperature, oil, and electrical readings) to assess real-time machine health to predict machine failure before it occurs. Condition-based monitoring is like putting a health tracker on industrial equipment.
